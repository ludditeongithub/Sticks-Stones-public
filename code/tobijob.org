#+title: Tobijob
The terms of the agreement is that Oluwatobi Macaulay will be paid 12.21 Great British Pounds per hour after she has checked in and then checked out using Element or Discord, where the time she has checked in and out will be noted. If she forgets to check in, the amount of work she has done will be analyzed and she will be paid for the estimated hours, but she must notify the manager by message in Element of this request and it is possible that that tabulation is only added to her next payment period instead of the current payment period. If she forgets to check out of a shift than the minimum amount of time based on the completed work, or the minimum shift duration, 2 hours, will be paid. The maximum allowed shift is 10 hours, and if a shift has ended there must be at least an hour period between check out and check in if less than 4 hours, two hour period if more than 4 hours but less than 8 hours, or an 8 hour period if more than 8 hours. The employee has the right to do a lunch break check in, also through Element, where her accumulation of payment will be paused until she does a lunch break check out. However, if she forgets to do a lunch break check out, the employer has the right to pay her only for the duration between the shift check in and lunch break check in. Including the lunch break, the maximum shift time cannot be more than 13 hours.

The hours worked during the week, before Friday 8PM PST,  will be delivered to Oluwatobi to a bank account of her choice weekly on Saturday no later than 8PM PST.

The nature of the work that Oluwatobi is expected to complete is creating pixel art. Secondarily, she will also be paid for conducting research regarding pixel art aesthetics and stragtegies, sales and advertissing of indie games. However, for both of these secondary tasks must be recorded in Element or Discord, if she wishes to have this work tabulated, and the employer is able to deny paying her for these secondary tasks, though they must also notify Oluwatobi in advance of her check in if this is the case.

If Oluwatobi submits no pixel art, or no reports regarding her research progress before or within an hour of checking out, the employer has the right to assume that she did not perform her tasks and deny her payment.
Due to the safe nature of the work, the employee will not be compensated or insured for any workplace injuries between their check in and check out time.

The employer has the right to terminate the contract with one day notice. Oluwatobi will still receive her accumlulated funds by the first upcoming Saturday no later than 8PM PST. Unless the employer terminates the contract, these terms last until July 27th 2025.
