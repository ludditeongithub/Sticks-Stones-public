* Mechanics
** Paradoxes
*** Laws
- all agents which have traveled through time cannot be prevented from travelling through time in the past.
- agents cannot prevent their own birth, nor the birth of anyone else who were crucial to them travelling through time or being born.
*** Level
Violating a paradox law does not necessarily end the level right there and then. The paradox level will be advanced based on the severity of the violation. A level can only finish in victory with paradox level 0, but the paradox level can be higher as long as it does not reach the max level. There are consequences to higher paradox levels though (perhaps enemies coming in).
** Time Machine
The time machine has a limited amount of time gas. There are different flavors of time machine which benefit or restrict the player in certain ways.
** Replication Machine
The replication machine allows an agent to clone themselves. This can be done to solve tasks which require more bodies that the player currently has under their control.
There are replication machines of different strength which can clone different agents. Certain machines will reject replicants. The main mechanic is that the player must kill all copies of an agent except the original to fix the timeline, but also killing a certain agent can create paradoxes.
** Memory Codes and Memory Reader
Certain memories may need to exist for certain events to take place, even if a replicant has been made.
