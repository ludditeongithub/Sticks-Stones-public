** Item Levels
Every resource that can be used as the main resource in making gear items determines the "item level". The item level is what is checked when required items are checked, anything equal to or above the set item level is okay. Often an item level is skipped. There are several item level hierarchies; items (usually placeables) have their own separate item level hierarchy.

Strength/Brittleness Item Levels:
1. Wood
2. Bone
3. Stone
4. Copper
5. Bronze
6. Iron
7. Steel

Structure Durability Levels:
1. Sticks/Wattle
2. Cob
3. Brick
4. Stone
5. Bronze
6. Iron
7. Steel

Beauty Item Levels:
0. Dirt/Wattle
1. Iron/Clay/Cob
2. Stone
3. Bone
4. Steel
5. Brick/Wood
6. Copper/Marble
7. Bronze
8. Gemstones (Lower)
9. Silver
10. Gold
11. Gemstones (Upper)
