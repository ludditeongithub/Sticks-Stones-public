\subsection{Environment Ambience Noise}
\label{sec:orge4248ec}
This is the average background noise that is always playing when the player's camera is in that biome-- it should not jarring or overbearing, and it must seamlessly be able to flow into the sounds when the player moves the camera from one biome to another. I recommend combining the most similar biomes: one for 'plains', one for 'forests', one for tundra, one for coastal areas and one for deserts.  From there we can test, layer and specify for the individual biomes. For now, avoid the sounds of non-bug animals like owls and wolves, since those are separate animals.\\

also, to be separately applied:\\
\begin{itemize}
\item[{$\square$}] gentle river noise\\
\item[{$\square$}] gentle wind noise\\
\end{itemize}
\subsection{Unit noises}
\label{sec:org6184ef8}
\subsubsection{Movement}
\label{sec:org51970b0}
We're keeping these minimal for now. A gentle walking sound, one on grass/sand, one on hard stone, should be fine, and a running sound for each of those. Keep in mind this will play when a player has selected a Unit who is walking/running.\\

a generic patter noise that can be used for animals is also good.\\
